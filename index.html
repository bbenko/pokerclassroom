Controls
Keyboard shortcuts: Esc to fold, Space to check or call, Enter to bet or raise the selected amount. After a hand ends, Space, Esc, or Enter deals the next hand. Shortcuts are inactive while typing a bet amount. Choose a preset, All-in, or a custom total, then press Bet or Raise to confirm. Preflop presets use big blinds for opening bets and multiples of the current bet for reraises. Postflop presets use a fraction of the pot after calling. Raise sizes are the total bet for this street. The button rotates and stacks reset each hand. Mixed stacks give you 100 BB against opponents with 25–150 BB.
