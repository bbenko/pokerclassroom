About & controls

Poker Classroom is an open-source, six-player no-limit Texas Hold’em trainer that runs in your browser. Play against five computer opponents, choose when to fold, check, call, bet, or raise, and review feedback on your decisions.


Turn on Show coach to see guidance as you play. Use the opening range chart to explore starting hands, and expand Hand history to review or export your session. Your session is saved in this browser. The dealer button rotates and stacks reset after each hand, so you can keep practicing fresh situations.


View the code on GitHub to explore how the game and coach work.
